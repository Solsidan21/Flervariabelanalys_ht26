**Anvisningar:** Endast penna, blyerts och radergummi. Inga räknare eller grafiska hjälpmedel. Skriv på papper som tillhandahålls, endast på ena sidan. Märk varje sida med uppgiftsnummer, högst en lösning per sida. Skriv tydligt och ge korta men klara motiveringar. Använd figurer där det passar.

### Uppgift 1
Compute the Taylor polynomial at 0 of the second degree to the function f(x, y) = 2x + y + 2 cos(x + 2y), and then use this to approximate f(0.1, 0.2).

### Uppgift 2
A person walks around on a road on a hill with the shape f(x, y) = 30 - (x + 2)^2 - y^2. The projection of the road on the xy-plane is the unit circle.
(a) Which are the points of steepest ascent/descent, and how steep is it there?
(b) At the point (1, 0, 21), the person is finally fed up with his activity and decides to head towards the pub, located straight north-east. What is the slope in this direction at the given point?

### Uppgift 3
An infinite size egg carton is given by z = 5 cos(x + y) + 3 sin(2(x - y)). Determine all critical points and classify them (local/global min/max, saddle point etc.)

### Uppgift 4
Consider the double integral

    doubleintegral over D (x - 2y)/(x^2 + y^2)^2 dx dy.

Compute it if:
(a) D is defined by x >= 1, y >= 1.
(b) D is defined by |(x, y)| >= 2.

### Uppgift 5
(a) Compute the length of the curve parametrized by r(t) = (t^3, t^2, 3t^4) for 0 <= t <= 1.
    [OBS: kurshemsidan anger att denna deluppgift innehåller ett fel och inte är lösbar explicit.]
(b) Compute the area of the surface z = 2 sqrt(1 - x^2 - y^2) above the unit disc.

### Uppgift 6
Consider the surface S: 2x + y^3 = 3yz.
(a) For most points P = (x_0, y_0, z_0) on S this surface defines z as a function of x, y near (x_0, y_0). For which points does this fail?
(b) For which points as above can you not solve for y as a function of x, z?
(c) Show that the answer to (b) is parametrized by r(t) = (t^3, t, t^2). (In particular show that this curve lies in S.)

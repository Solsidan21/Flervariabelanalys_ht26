**Anvisningar:** Endast penna, blyerts och radergummi. Inga räknare eller grafiska hjälpmedel. Skriv på papper som tillhandahålls, endast på ena sidan. Märk varje sida med uppgiftsnummer, högst en lösning per sida. Skriv tydligt och ge korta men klara motiveringar. Använd figurer där det passar.

### Uppgift 1
Consider the connected body between the surfaces z = cos r and z = sin r that includes the point (0, 0, 1/2), where r = sqrt(x^2 + y^2).
(a) Draw it. (1p)
(b) Compute its volume by first integrating in z and then in (x, y). (2p)
(c) Write up, as far as you can, the formula for computing the volume using the slice method (i.e. reversing the order of integration in (b)). (2p)

### Uppgift 2
Consider the graph of the function f(x, y) = e^(5 - x^2 - y^2).
(a) Which is the direction of maximum decrease at the point (2, 1)? (1p)
(b) Write a formula for the tangent plane at the point (2, 1). (2p)
(c) What is the Hessian for f at the point (2, 1)? (1p)
(d) Write a formula for the second order Taylor approximation at the point (2, 1). (1p)

### Uppgift 3
Compute the area of the piece of the surface z = 1 + y^2 which lies above the set |x| <= y <= 1.

### Uppgift 4
(a) Consider the equation

    e^x + xy + e^y - 2 = u
    x^3 - x + y + y^3 = v

where u, v are new variables. Show that x, y can be written as C^1-functions of u, v near (u, v) = (0, 0). (2p)
(b) Compute x_u, x_v, y_u and y_v at (0, 0). (2p)
(c) Apparently x = y = 0 is a solution to the equation system

    e^x + xy + e^y = 2
    x^3 - x + y + y^3 = 0

Show that in a sufficiently small neighborhood of (0, 0) in the xy-plane there is no other solution. (1p)

### Uppgift 5
Let F : R^2 -> R be defined by

    F(x, y) = xy(x - y)/(x^2 + y^2)  for (x, y) != (0, 0),
    F(0, 0) = 0.

(a) (2p) Show that F is not continuous at (0, 0).
(b) (1p) Compute F_1(x, y) and F_2(x, y) for all (x, y).
(c) (1p) Compute F_12(0, 0) and F_21(0, 0).
(d) (1p) Why isn't F_12(0, 0) = F_21(0, 0)?

### Uppgift 6
The function f : R^2 -> R given by f(x_1, x_2) = (1/4)(|x_1| + |x_2|)^2 - (1/4)(x_1^2 + x_2^2) is of interest in optimization theory.
(a) Find its critical and singular points. (2p)
(b) Find the global minimum. (1p)
(c) Given a point y in R^2, the so-called "proximal operator" prox_f : R^2 -> R^2 computes the minimum of f(x) + (1/2)||x - y||^2. Compute prox_f(1, 2). (2p)

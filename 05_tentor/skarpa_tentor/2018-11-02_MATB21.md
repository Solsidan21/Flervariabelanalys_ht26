### Uppgift 1
(5p) Compute the volume of the solid between the cone z = 4 - sqrt(x^2 + y^2) and the paraboloid z = x^2 + y^2 + 2.

### Uppgift 2
Consider the equation

    cos(z) + x^3 + (1/4)y^4 - y = 1.

(a) (2p) What is the equation of the tangent plane to this level surface at the point (x, y, z) = (1, 0, pi/2)?
(b) (3p) Around which points can we be certain that the solutions (x, y, z) to the above equation can be expressed as (x, y(x, z), z) and (x(y, z), y, z), where y(x, z) and x(y, z) are C^1-functions?

### Uppgift 3
(a) (4p) Find the extreme values of

    f(x, y) = x^2 + y^2 + sin(x^2) + cos(y^2)

over the region x^2 + y^2 <= pi.
(b) (1p) Let f : R^2 -> R be a function on a region D subset R^2. What properties of D and f guarantee the existence of a maximum and a minimum? Motivate why f and D in (a) satisfy the hypotheses of this result.

### Uppgift 4
(a) (4p) Transform the partial differential equation

    y (partial u/partial x) - x (partial u/partial y) = x + y

by using the change of variables x = r cos(theta) and y = r sin(theta) for theta in [0, 2pi] and r > 0.
Hint: Observe that x^2 + y^2 = r^2 and tan(theta) = y/x.
(b) (1p) Solve this partial differential equation.

### Uppgift 5
Let I be the following improper integral

    I = doubleintegral over D ( x^10 y^9 - y sin(x y^9) + x^2 + 1/sqrt(1 - x^2 - y^2) ) dA,

where D = {(x, y) in R^2 : x^2 + y^2 < 1}.

(a) (1p) Why is it true that

    I = doubleintegral over D x^10 y^9 dA - doubleintegral over D y sin(x y^9) dA
        + doubleintegral over D x^2 dA + doubleintegral over D 1/sqrt(1 - x^2 - y^2) dA ?

(b) (4p) Determine the convergence of I. If it is convergent, compute its value.
Hint: Use the splitting in part (a) and look for symmetry.

### Uppgift 6
(a) (3p) Compute F'(x) if F is

    F(x) = integral(0 to x) (x - y) f(y) dy.

(b) (1p) What condition on f permits differentiation inside the integral performed in (a)?
(c) (1p) Let

    F_n(x) = integral(0 to x) ((x - y)^(n-1) / (n-1)!) f(y) dy,   n in N.

Compute F_n^(n), i.e. the n-th derivative of F_n.

**Anvisningar:** Endast penna, blyerts och radergummi. Inga räknare eller grafiska hjälpmedel. Skriv på papper som tillhandahålls, endast på ena sidan. Märk varje sida med uppgiftsnummer, högst en lösning per sida. Skriv tydligt och ge korta men klara motiveringar. Använd figurer där det passar.

### Uppgift 1
(a) Explain where the factor r comes from when changing variables to polar coordinates in R^2. In particular, give an intuitive explanation why it is natural that the angle theta does not appear. (2p)

(b) Compute

    doubleintegral over E arctan(sqrt(x^2 + y^2)) dA

where E = {(x, y) : 1 <= x^2 + y^2 <= 3, x/sqrt(3) <= y <= sqrt(3) x}. (3p)

### Uppgift 2
A cinnamon bun flies along the trajectory (t, t^2, (2/3)t^3), starting at time t = 0.

(a) What is the speed and velocity at any given point t = t_0? (1p)
(b) At t = 1 the bun runs out of fuel and continues into space along a straight line with constant speed. What is the formula for its trajectory for t > 1? (1p)
(c) What is the distance traveled between t = 0 and t = 1? (2p)
Hint for (c): Along the path! (You obviously won't get 3 points by writing sqrt(1^2 + 1^2 + (2/3)^2).)

### Uppgift 3
(a) Suppose that f is a function such that grad f(1, 1, 1) = (5, 2, 1), and let x(t) = (t^2, t^(-3), t). Find (d/dt) f(x(t)) at t = 1. (2p)
(b) Find the equation of the tangent plane to the function g(x, y) = x / sqrt(x^2 + y^2) at the point P = (3, -4, 3/5) on the graph. (2p)
(c) What is the normal line through P? (1p)

### Uppgift 4
Consider the "change of variables" s = 2x^3 + 3y^2 and t = x on R^2, and the differential equation

    y (partial u/partial x) = x^2 (partial u/partial y),   u in C^2.

(a) Is this really a change of variables, i.e. is it a bijection? (1p)
(b) Show that it is a bijection from the half plane y > 0 into a set in the (s, t)-plane, and draw this set. (1p)
(c) Solve the differential equation for y > 0, by using the above change of variables. (3p)

### Uppgift 5
Find the surface area of the cone of height h obtained by rotating the line z = 3x around the z-axis in R^3. (3p)

### Uppgift 6
Find the maximum and the minimum of the function

    f(x, y) = (x^2 + 2y^2) e^(-x^2 + y^2)

(a) ...in the unit disc. (3p)
(b) ...in the plane R^2. (2p)

### Uppgift 7
For which alpha in R do we have

    lim_((x,y)->0) x|y|^alpha / (x^2 + y^4) = 0 ?   (3p)

Hint: Expand (a - b)^2 to conclude that 2ab <= a^2 + b^2.
